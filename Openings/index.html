About the university and school

The Ohio State University is a member of the Association of American Universities (AAU) and one of the nation’s leading public research institutions. Ohio State ranks among the top research universities in the United States, with strong programs in science and engineering.

The School of Earth Sciences at Ohio State conducts research across four core themes: Earth History, Geodetic Science, Solid Earth Dynamics, and Water, Climate, and the Environment. The School offers undergraduate degrees in Earth Sciences and graduate degrees in Earth Sciences and Geodetic Science. Explore our courses, certificates and degrees at our website.
